Tool profiles

Scripture Forge

Generates a first-pass draft of a Bible book from a team's own translation work, delivered into Paratext.

Faithbridge

Answers a translator's questions about a passage by searching a curated set of translation resources.

Scribe

An offline editor for recording, checking and re-recording oral Bible translation. No AI.

AQuA

Flags the places in a draft most likely to need a checker's attention, against a reference translation.

Paratext Checking Assistant

Reads a draft inside Paratext and raises findings on meaning and naturalness for a reviewer to judge.
