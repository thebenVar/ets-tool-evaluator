Two questions before scoring

A tool has to pass both to be worth assessing. If it fails the first, say so and stop: naming a tool as unassessed is a better outcome than scoring it on guesswork.

How close are you to this tool?

Anyone who builds it, supports it, or has a stake in it being adopted should say so here, and should not be the only person scoring it. Declaring closeness is not a disqualification; hiding it is.

Fourteen criteria

For each one: write down what you actually know and where it came from, then choose the description that fits. Where you do not know enough, say so — that is a real answer, and four of these cannot be answered at all without talking to people who use the tool. The number is shorthand; the reasoning is the finding. Click i beside any criterion for a fuller explanation of what it asks and what to look for.
